## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning

The objective of this keyword research is to optimize the portfolio for discovery by **technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, and founders** seeking candidates with demonstrable, production-ready AI application development skills.

### Strategic Shift: "Prompting" to "Production Engineering"
- **Previous Framing:** "AI-powered creative workflows", "prompt engineering", generic web design.
- **Validated Opportunity:** In the current 2026 technical job market, hiring managers are actively filtering out "notebook-only" or "prompt-wrapper" candidates. Recruiters prioritize engineers who demonstrate:
  1. **System Reliability & Determinism:** Handling non-deterministic LLM outputs with Pydantic schemas and guardrails.
  2. **Evaluation & Automated Testing:** Concrete test suites (e.g., unit, integration, RAGAS evals) rather than vague claims of accuracy.
  3. **Backend Integration:** FastAPI, Celery, FFmpeg, asynchronous queues, and PostgreSQL.
  4. **Domain Realism:** Addressing code-switching, rate limits, audio latency, and API degradation.

## 2. Methodology & Evidence Rules

- **Research Period:** October 2026.
- **Methodology:** Direct Google SERP inspection for exact-match and long-tail variants across India and Kochi search contexts. Evaluated first-page intent (hiring managers searching for portfolios vs. job seekers searching for listings) and analyzed competitor ranking assets.
- **Strict Evidence Standards:**
  - **Zero Fabrication:** Third-party paid SEO metrics (such as Semrush/Ahrefs search volume and Keyword Difficulty KD) are **intentionally omitted** because live API subscriptions were not accessible in this sandbox environment.
  - Competitive viability is graded qualitatively based on the domain authority and content depth of currently ranking URLs.
  - Queries where page 1 is completely dominated by job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn, Naukri) are categorized as **candidate-seeking-jobs** and rejected for portfolio targeting.

## 5. Strategic Takeaways

1. **Focus on "Application Engineering" over "AI Theory":** Emphasize connecting APIs, runtime schema enforcement, async workers, and handling failure modes.
2. **Surface Concrete Numbers Where Verified:** Showcase verified test counts (e.g., 1,208 tests in iScribe, 171 media pipeline tests in PromptToShort) rather than generic claims of "high performance".
3. **Prevent Keyword Cannibalization:** Assign distinct primary targets to distinct routes:
   - Homepage: Broad applied AI & software identity.
   - `/work`: Engineering case studies index.
   - `/work/iscribe`: Clinical NLP testing & evaluation.
   - `/work/rshorts`: Python asynchronous media pipeline engineering.
   - `/about`: Professional background & regional developer profile.
   - `/services`: Technical web development & SEO architecture.
