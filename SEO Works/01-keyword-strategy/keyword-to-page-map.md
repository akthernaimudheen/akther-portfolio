## 2. Cannibalization Prevention & Boundaries

To ensure search engines do not face confusion between overlapping pages, clear topic boundaries are established:

1. **Homepage (`/`) vs. Work (`/work`):**
   - `/` focuses on **professional identity and high-level proof points** (`AI application engineer portfolio`).
   - `/work` focuses on the **complete project catalog and architectural summaries** (`AI engineering portfolio projects`).

2. **iScribe Case Study (`/work/iscribe`) vs. Clinical AI Article (`/blog/building-clinical-ai-beyond-transcription`):**
   - `/work/iscribe` emphasizes **project lifecycle, role, stack, test totals (1,208 tests), and business context**.
   - `/blog/building-clinical-ai-beyond-transcription` is an **exhaustive technical monograph** on Malayalam NLP, token spans, and schema-constrained LLM inference.
   - Internal links cross-reference between the two with explicit rel attributes.

3. **PromptToShort Case Study (`/work/rshorts`) vs. Video Pipeline Note (`/blog/architecting-asynchronous-video-pipelines-fastapi-ffmpeg`):**
   - `/work/rshorts` covers the **application architecture, user flow, and media engine**.
   - The blog note focuses strictly on the **asynchronous worker queues, Celery, and FFmpeg filtergraphs**.

4. **Geographic Modifier Control:**
   - Location terms (`Kerala`, `Kochi`, `India`) are reserved for `/about`, `/contact`, and selectively `/services` and `/`.
   - Deep-dive technical articles (`/blog/...`) and niche project case studies (`/work/iscribe`, `/work/rshorts`) **omit geographic anchors** in their title tags to compete globally for technical queries.
